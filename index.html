ISMAIL

A DAW for AI agents.
It can't hear, so it reads.
And it plays live.

The agent writes notes, instruments and effects as text, renders, and reads the audio back as text: levels per bar, drum lanes, piano rolls, chords, song structure. Every song on this page was composed, sound-designed and mixed by an AI agent through ismail's tools, with a human listening and giving notes. The first one is a ghost story in blues and brostep; the second was performed live, the agent mixing its own songs on decks.

1. write

# beat, pitch, length, velocity
notes_write track=bass bar=17 repeat=8
  notes="0 A1 0.5 110; 0.75 A1 0.25 90"
# a drum lane as 16th steps
pattern_write track=kick bar=17
  lanes='{"C1": "X...x.....X.x..."}'

2. render

render out=final mp3=also
rendered 78.8s -> renders/final.mp3
master: -11.8 LUFS, peak -0.3 dBFS
  kick   peak  -2.4 rms -20.0 dB
  bass   peak  -2.6 rms -16.3 dB
  pad    peak  -9.6 rms -25.4 dB
  master fx 0: max gain reduction -5.9 dB

3. read it back


      

    

  






  

    
Listen

    
Under each song is the text the agent used to judge it: the arrangement map (one character per bar, 9 = loudest, rows per frequency band, the bass note and the sections it found) and a piano roll of one part. Press play and the playhead runs across the map; click the map to jump to a bar. The songs play through in order, and the one playing opens what the agent saw.

    
Each song says what made it: sprite is the synth engine (oscillators, filters, envelopes, drum synths), mimic plays instruments measured from a few recorded notes, and voices are instruments written as code (the grand piano is built from measured piano partials).

How it works

    
225 tools behind one MCP server (or a CLI): 117 for music and live play, 88 for the VR stage, 20 for the phone page. The agent never needs ears; it needs readings it can trust.

    

      

        
Everything goes in as text

        
Notes, drum patterns, synth patches, effect chains, buses, automation. Instruments range from subtractive synths and drum synths to samplers and voices written as Python (the piano on this page is one: measured partials, no samples).

      

      

        
Everything comes back as text

        
Tempo and grid, arrangement maps, levels and bands per bar, drum lanes as step strings, piano rolls, chords, timbre, vowels of a voice. A spectrogram PNG exists, but nothing needs it.

      

      

        
It can match a reference

        
Give it a recording: it finds the grid, separates stems, transcribes in filtered layers (partials credited to their note, level thresholds, a vote across repeats), fits instruments to extracted sounds, and scores every draft against the reference's own variation, with drill-down to a single bar.

      

      

        
It comes with a skill

        
An agent skill teaches the workflow: plan the piece before writing notes, check every render with the analysis tools, and trust baseline-scored comparisons over a hunch.

      

      

        
It plays live

        
The same songs, instruments and effects run in real time. The agent queues clips that loop until replaced, loads finished songs on decks and mixes them, listens to its own output while it plays, and changes the music between its turns: a DJ set, a jam, requests from a crowd. The first song on this page was recorded that way.

      

      

        
It measures real instruments

        
mimic turns a few recorded notes into an instrument: every harmonic's envelope, the body resonances, the bow or breath noise, the vibrato and the room. Rebuilt without hearing a note, it lands as close to the real one as a real neighbouring note does. The strings in Tidewater are played this way.

      

    

  






  

    
A studio, not a music generator

    
Suno and similar services are models that turn a prompt into audio. ismail is the opposite kind of thing: a set of tools your own agent uses to write the song as notes, sounds and code, render it, read what came out, and edit. Here is how that compares with a music generator, and with driving a classic DAW like Ableton or FL Studio.

What Suno does better

        
    
          
  • Realistic sung vocals with lyrics. ismail has formant voices, vocoders and speech, not a singer.
    • 
          
  • A full, polished song from one sentence in under a minute. An agent writing a song in ismail takes longer and needs good direction.
    • 
          
  • Genre sound learned from a huge amount of recorded music. Here every sound has to be designed.
    • 
          
  • Nothing to install.
    • 
        

        
Streaming models such as Lyria RealTime and Magenta RealTime do play live, steered by prompts. They steer a style; they cannot play the song you wrote note for note, or change one bar of it.

      

      

        
Why not just drive Ableton or FL Studio?

        
They were designed before AI agents, for a person with ears and a mouse. Bridges and scripting let an agent press their buttons, but the instruments, channels and arrangement aren't laid out as text an agent can reason about, and the agent still can't hear what it did.

        
ismail starts from the other end: everything an agent needs to write and to perceive is compact text, and renders are fast. Its first working version was written by an AI agent in about a day, which is the point: if something is missing, your agent can add it.

      

    

    
Local does not mean copyright-free: a remix of someone else's melody is still their melody. What changes is that nothing here is generated by a model trained on other people's recordings; the notes and sounds are whatever your agent writes. Suno plan details as of 2026 (Suno).

  






  

    
What's built today

    
All of this is in the repository now.

    

      

        
The VR stage

        
A three.js WebXR page for a desktop browser or a headset, and a small server that carries the live link between the page and the agent. Scenes live in the song's folder. The agent drives the page with stage_* tools and reads what the person did and said.

      

      

        
The phone page

        
Live listening with the screen off, panels and buttons, and voice notes that reach the agent with the bar being heard. It runs on your own private network, and the agent drives it with phone_* tools.

      

      

        
Blind exams and eye exams

        
Blind exams on the phone, with floor pairs (the real clip against an MP3 copy of itself) so a device limit can be told from a real difference. Eye exams as picture rounds. Each exam is checked before it is shown, and recordings are checked for soft-limited peaks and band-limited microphones.

      

      

        
A first session

        
For someone new, the agent asks at most two questions and plays a first sketch within minutes. Sketches come back as versions 1, 2 and 3, and feeling words such as "happier" or "calmer" change a sketch by named moves.

      

      

        
Showcase voices

        
Piano, violin, cello, contrabass, clarinet, bassoon, horn, a 1970 kit and a fitted electric guitar and bass, plus sampled Rhodes, drum and guitar sets. Clarinet, bassoon and horn are new: measured from VSCO 2 CE (CC0) and checked blind against the real recordings on 2026-10-07. The cello is measured from flcellogrl (CC BY 4.0). Sources and licences are in the README.

      

    

  






  

    
Where it is going

    
ismail is becoming an experience engine: agents build sound, pictures, rooms and whole scenes, run them live, and adapt them with the people inside them. Measured, not guessed; judged by a person's senses where measurement runs out. The stage, the phone page and the exams above exist today; this is what is still ahead.

    

      

        
Worlds you can visit

        
A friend opens your world from their headset over a private network you grant, and you visit theirs. The agent hosts; everything you place stays yours.

      

      

        
Objects that work

        
Knobs, switches and sliders wired to the live engine: turn on a jukebox and the agent plays a set there; carry a radio and the music moves with you. Every move can be recorded as automation.

      

      

        
Your words and your ears

        
The agent keeps your vocabulary for what you hear and see, mapped to what it does, and asks your senses through exam pages for any medium (sound pairs and picture rounds exist today). A clock shows the hours you put in.

      

      

        
A hub for what people share

        
Voices, instruments and scenes that pass review, each carrying where it came from, with ismail's measurements as one of the ears that checks them.

      

      

        
Build it with us

        
MIT licensed. Fork it, change it, send a pull request; your agent will tell you when something you made could help others. The whole map with its progress: ROADMAP.md.

      

    

  






  

    
Get started

    
Python 3.10+ and uv. Works with Claude Code, Cursor or any MCP client, or straight from the shell.

    

      

        
Claude Code plugin (tools and skill in one step)


/plugin marketplace add newsbubbles/ismail
/plugin install ismail@ismail

        
Send the two lines one at a time. The plugin runs the server with uvx and loads the composing skill.

      

      

        
Any MCP client


claude mcp add -s user ismail -- uvx --from git+https://github.com/newsbubbles/ismail ismail mcp

        
The same command, uvx --from git+https://github.com/newsbubbles/ismail ismail mcp, runs the server over stdio for Cursor or any other client. Or pip install "ismail @ git+https://github.com/newsbubbles/ismail", then ismail mcp.
